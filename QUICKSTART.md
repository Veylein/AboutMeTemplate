# 🚀 Quick Start in 3 Steps

**Never coded before? No problem! Follow these 3 simple steps:**

## Step 1: Download the Template

1. Click the green **"Code"** button at the top of this page
2. Click **"Download ZIP"**
3. Extract the ZIP file anywhere on your computer

## Step 2: Edit Your Information

1. Open `index.html` with **Notepad** (Windows) or **TextEdit** (Mac)
2. Look for lines that say `<!-- CUSTOMIZE: ... -->`
3. Change only the text inside these sections:
   - Replace "Your Name Here" with your actual name
   - Replace "YOUR_USERNAME" with your social media usernames
   - Write your own bio

**Example:**
```html
Change this:    <h1 class="name">Your Name Here</h1>
To this:        <h1 class="name">Sarah Johnson</h1>
```

## Step 3: Open and Share

1. Find `index.html` on your computer
2. **Double-click** it to open in your web browser
3. See your new About Me page!
4. To share online, follow the "How to Publish" section in README.md

## 🎨 Want to Change Colors?

Open `style.css` and find line 16. Change this color code to any you like:

```css
--accent-color: #667eea;  ← Change this!
```

**Popular color codes:**
- Pink: `#ed64a6`
- Blue: `#4299e1`
- Green: `#48bb78`
- Purple: `#805ad5`
