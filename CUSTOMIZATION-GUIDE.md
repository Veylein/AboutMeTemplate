# 📋 Quick Customization Checklist

Use this checklist to customize your About Me page step by step!

## ✅ Basic Information

- [ ] Change your name (index.html, line 37)
- [ ] Update your tagline/title (index.html, line 38)
- [ ] Write your bio (index.html, lines 45-51)
- [ ] Update footer with your name (index.html, line 166)

## 🖼️ Visual Elements

- [ ] Add your profile picture (index.html, line 34)
  - Upload your image to the same folder
  - Or use an image URL from the internet
  - Recommended size: 400x400 pixels or larger (square)

## 🔗 Social Media Links

Delete the entire `<a>` tag for any platform you don't want to display.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Profile Picture Tips:**
   - Use a square image (same width and height)
   - At least 400x400 pixels for best quality
   - JPG or PNG format
   - Keep file size under 1MB

2. **Writing Your Bio:**
   - Keep it concise (2-4 sentences)
   - Show your personality
   - Use emojis to add character 😊
   - Mention what you do or what you're passionate about

3. **Social Media:**
   - Only include platforms you're active on
   - Make sure usernames are correct (test the links!)
   - Order them by importance to you

4. **Colors:**
   - Choose colors that represent you
   - Make sure text is readable (good contrast)
   - Stick to 2-3 main colors for a cohesive look
